# WHERE

The upper right corner of the SuiteCRM dashboard shows the username under a profile icon.  
Click username, "Admin", "Role Management", click the name of the role to access the role matrix.

# HOW

Click "Access" and select "Disabled" from the dropdown menu that appears.

Click "Delete", "Export", "Import", "Mass Update" and set each to "None".

Click "Edit", "List", "View" and set each to "Owner".

Set the “Access” of the following modules to “Enabled”:

- Calls
- Calls Reschedule
- Campaigns
- Email - Templates
- Email Marketing
- Emails
- Knowledge Base
- Leads
- Meetings
- Opportunities
- Outbound Email Accounts
- Reports
- Survey Question Options
- Survey Question Responses
- Survey Questions
- Survey Responses
- Surveys
- Targets
- Targets - Lists
- WorkFlow

Click **SAVE** to continue.
