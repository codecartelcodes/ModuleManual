# Pick the Type

When recruiting a Motivator:

- Ask them to take the **OETJS personality typing survey**.  
- The survey helps indicate the **behavior patterns** of the Motivator.  
- Certain personality types may correlate with higher sales performance, e.g., **ENTJ, ENFJ, ENFP**.  
- Strategy: Select Motivators whose natural behaviors fit the requirements of the role.  

**Action:**  
- Collect the **results chart** from the candidate for review.
