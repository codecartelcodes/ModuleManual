# Call the Competitors

Instead of focusing solely on teaching sales reps what your product does, prioritize equipping them with **competitive awareness**.

---

## Key Focus Areas

- Understand and communicate your product’s **advantages over competitors**  
- Be transparent about **potential drawbacks**  
- Provide **practical, real-world insights** to support conversations  

---

## Objective

Enable sales reps to:
- Have **informed, confident conversations** with customers  
- Address objections with clarity  
- Position the product effectively in a competitive market
