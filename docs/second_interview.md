## Core Philosophy

- A **10% more rigorous interview process** correlates with **2.6% higher employee satisfaction**
- Strong salespeople:
  - Do **not need months** to learn your product before selling  
  - Can begin selling while learning  
- Over-preparation without action = **lack of startup readiness**

---

## What Great Salespeople Do

- Make strong first impressions  
- Seek warm introductions through trusted networks  
- Conduct deep research before engagement  
- Build relationships immediately upon entering the environment  

---

## Key Interview Questions

### Introductory

- What was your role in a previous successful sales process?  
- How do you typically communicate with a prospect?  
- Describe a time you missed a sales quota. What did you learn?  
- What matters more: hitting quota or maintaining client satisfaction?  

---

### Behavioral & Integrity-Based

- How do you handle rejection?  
- Share an example of overcoming adversity  
- Describe a time you turned down a sale for ethical reasons  
- What is your core motivation (“Why”)?  
  - Chip on shoulder  
  - Mission-driven alignment  

---

### Values Validation

- Ask for **specific examples** where values influenced decisions  
- If answers lack depth or emotion:
  - Challenge the candidate  
  - Test for authenticity vs. performative answers  

---

## Evaluation Criteria

### Performance Metrics

- Quota achievement (Closed Won %)  
- Upselling KPIs  
- Customer relationships and support quality  

---

### Character & Traits

Ask yourself:

- Do they have **hustler DNA**?  
- Are they willing to do what it takes?  
- Do they show **entrepreneurial drive**?  
- Are they self-driven and ambitious?  
- Are they competitive and enjoy challenges?  
- Do they communicate well?  
- Do they have a **high tolerance for discomfort**?  
- Can they handle rejection?  
- Are they coachable?  
- Can they accept and apply feedback?  
- Are they both **competitive and compassionate**?  

---

## Interview Structure

### Interview #1
- Sales experience  
- Example of overcoming adversity  

### Interview #2
- Additional adversity example  
- Personality type assessment  

### Interview #3
- Final adversity validation  

---

## Reliability Test

- Require the candidate to **call in**
- Evaluate:
  - Punctuality  
  - Follow-through  
  - Professionalism  

---

## Strategic Positioning

- Identify candidate **life goals**
- Align opportunity with their vision  
- Consider offering:
  - Future management roles  
  - Partnership pathways  

---

## Sales Simulation

- Have the candidate **sell your service**
- Discuss:
  - Availability  
  - Approach  
  - Handling objections  

---

## Red Flags

- Overemphasis on preparation instead of action  
- Inability to provide real examples  
- Weak emotional connection to stated values  
- Lack of resilience or rejection tolerance  

---

## Guiding Principle

> Hire for attitude, not experience.
