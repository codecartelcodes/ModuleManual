# How to Recruit and Manage Motivators

## The Problems
Managing a Module’s **Motivators** is the most difficult challenge.  
Motivators are **commission-only sales representatives**, and sales reps tend to have **high attrition rates**.

Balancing **leads and Motivators** is critical:
- Too few qualified leads → Motivators cannot make enough sales.  
- Too many Motivators → Not enough leads per Motivator.  
- Too few Motivators → Missed sales opportunities.  

### Why manage Motivators?
- Multiple Motivators are needed to **scale and reach $1M in revenue**.  
- Thousands of sales at $99–$199 are required to reach $1M.  
- Phone-based Motivators **convert 20–25% of high-quality inbound leads**, improving revenue efficiency.  
- Commission-only Motivators support a **black financial model**, keeping costs aligned with revenue.

---

## The Solutions

### 1. Lead by Example
- Before recruiting Motivators, the **Manager should generate leads and make at least 6 sales**.  
- If a Motivator is unavailable, the Manager **should make sales themselves**.  
- A Manager with real sales experience **instills confidence** in the Motivators.  
- Motivators must see that making sales is **achievable and not difficult**.

### 2. Avoid Hiring Recruiters
- Third-party recruiters charge hundreds to thousands of dollars to post ads and screen candidates.  
- Results are **basic** and do not guarantee better performance than in-house recruiting.  
- Best use of funds: pay for **advertising to attract sales reps directly**.  
- Exceptions: companies that **recruit, screen, and manage** reps.  
- Core responsibility: **Master ongoing recruiting** yourself.

### 3. Share the Vision
- Share the Module’s mission or cause with Motivators.  
- While **money motivates**, a meaningful purpose **increases loyalty and retention**.

### 4. Communicate Commissions
- Motivators are the **highest paid team members**.  
- Suggested rate: **50% of gross revenue**.  
- Explain the **black financial model** so they understand staying with your Module is more profitable than starting their own.  

#### Upsell Incentives
- Motivators earn **double commission on upsells**.  
- This incentivizes **relationship maintenance** with Members.  
- Upsells are easier due to the **existing warm relationship**.  
- Higher pay rewards **loyalty and consistent performance**.

### 5. Show the Support
- Leads are **new, inbound, and pre-qualified**.  
- Marketing team (Messengers) generates leads via the **capture page**, which filters potential customers.  
- Motivator’s role: **answer questions, build relationships, close sales**.

### 6. Monitor Performance
- Target **20–25% conversion** of inbound leads.  
- Reward top performers by **assigning more leads**.

## Learn About Attrition
- **WHAT:** Motivators may leave frequently.  
- **HOW:** Track performance, identify trends, maintain a recruitment pipeline.  
- **WHY:** A Module is effectively a **recruiting business**; ongoing replacement is normal.
