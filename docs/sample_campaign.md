## Learn about Reselling
There are thousands of affiliate programs.

The programs include the infrastructure to sell products without a website. Please avoid developing a website to reduce development costs and the time it takes to start.

The landing pages have already been optimized and ranked based on conversion rate.

You decide which programs to promote to generate leads that will buy services inside StepsTEAM.com.

The products are successfully sold by amateurs without upfront pay. StepsTEAM, Inc uses the Mobe L Campaign to vet paid professionals. Once the campaign is self sustaining, StepsTEAM, Inc will hire the successful professionals to manage other campaigns that use longer term marketing strategies like link marketing, SEO, and content marketing.

## Setup the System
Please do the bare minimum when creating the remaining required infrastructure. If a website is needed, try to make it a simple brochure site with no logic. At most, capture customer email addresses. Since this campaign is off site, your agency can keep ownership rights to the graphics and written content.

---

## Find the Traffic
Make a list of 3 competitors to StepsTEAM. Use sites like [SimilarWeb.com](http://similarweb.com), [SpyFu.com](http://spyfu.com), and [Alexa.com](http://alexa.com) to determine where competitors get their traffic. There, you can see what sites are referring traffic to the competitor.

---

## Create the Ads
Click on “Show the Ads” on [SimilarWeb.com](https://www.similarweb.com/) to determine which ads are driving the most traffic to the competitors. Consider using [20dollarbanners.com](https://www.20dollarbanners.com/) to create a similar ad.

## Convert the Leads
Introduce StepsTEAM.com to the paying customer using email campaigns and drip campaigns. Explain that they can save time and effort by using StepsTEAM’s all inclusive step by step CRM. Rather than running the ads themselves and learning online marketing, they can simply hire our TEAM to manage over 2 dozen services for a very low cost. They also have the option of promoting their own StepsTEAM.com affiliate link as well.
