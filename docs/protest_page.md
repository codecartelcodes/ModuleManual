# Deep Research Assistant: Attack the uPlan

Use a Deep Research assistant to analyze the entire uPlan and test its assumptions with an emotionless, logic-driven large language model. This helps uncover hidden risks, weak assumptions, and gaps in the business model.

---

## 1. Deep Research Assistant
- **WHAT:** Use AI to stress-test your business plan.
- **HOW:** Ask the assistant to:
  - List all assumptions in the uPlan.
  - Identify logical flaws or inconsistencies.
  - Suggest worst-case scenarios.
- **WHY:** Reveals blind spots and improves decision-making.

---

## 2. Shred Ideas
- **WHAT:** Challenge ideas with a neutral, emotionless AI.
- **HOW:** Present your plan to the assistant and ask:
  - “What is the weakest part of this plan?”
  - “What could go wrong?”
  - “What woulan.
  - Identify log beat this?”
- **WHY:** Forces you to think critically and refine your strategy.

---

## 3. ATDC Investor Checklist Questions

### Business Model
- Is the business model ad-supported (in any way)?
- Does the financial model rely on tiny commissions?
- Is the business model business-to-consumer (B2C)?
- Does the business involve a two-sided marketplace?

### Product/Service
- Does the product or service create a new category?
- Are founders bootstrapping a venture-scale business?
- Are the founders creating a foundational AI model?
