# Call the Competitors

Shift the focus from simply explaining your product to building **competitive intelligence** within your sales team.

---

## Key Focus Areas

- Clearly articulate your product’s **advantages over competitors**  
- Acknowledge and understand **potential drawbacks**  
- Equip reps with **practical, real-world context** for conversations  

---

## Objective

Enable sales reps to:

- Communicate with **confidence and credibility**  
- Handle objections with **clarity and precision**  
- Effectively **position the product** within a competitive landscape
