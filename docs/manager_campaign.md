# Manager Campaign

---

## Overview

**StepsTEAM.com** is a customer relationship management (CRM) platform designed to help users build and maintain an affiliate business.

The website follows a **step-by-step sequence**:
- Each step covers a single topic  
- Pages are presented in order  
- Users can only move forward after completing each step  

As users progress, they:
- Learn the affiliate business model  
- Complete quizzes and registrations  
- Purchase products/services  
- Upgrade services to increase commissions

## Task Guide

### Keep It Simple
The business model includes complex, interconnected concepts. Focus on the core principle:

> **“Simply use StepsTEAM.com.”**

The platform is designed to guide users through everything in a structured, simplified way.

---

### Study the System
- Review the **Marketing Plan overview**
- Study the **Steps Sequence Document**, which includes:
  - Source materials  
  - Research  
  - Notes for each step  

Full access to these materials will be provided.

---

### See the Site
- Use the platform in **demo mode**
- Follow the instructions in red at the top of each page  
- This allows you to experience the flow without completing steps as a customer  

---

### Meet the Founder
- Schedule a **30-minute call** with **Drew Brown (Founder)**  
- Be prepared to confirm you have studied the system  
- Use the call to:
  - Ask questions  
  - Receive additional resources and white papers  

---

### Get an EIN
- Apply for an **Employer Identification Number (EIN)** if you do not already have one  
- Required to set up payment processing  

---

### Set Up PayPal
- Create a **PayPal Business Account (PayPal Express)**  
- No monthly fee required  
- Payments will be sent via PayPal API  

---

### Send the Credentials
Provide the following to Drew Brown:
- PayPal email (ID)  
- Client ID  
- Client Secret  

This enables direct commission payments.

---

### Obey the Law

**Compliance Guidelines:**
- Do **not** register your EIN-based company with the affiliate program used in the steps  
- Avoid income claims (earnings/profit promises)  
- Avoid statements like “no work required”  
- Only describe actual services provided
