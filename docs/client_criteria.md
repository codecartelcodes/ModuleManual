# Module Eligibility Criteria for Startups

To work with a **Module**, a startup should meet these requirements:

- **Service‑based business**  
  Offers services (not just product‑only businesses).

- **Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV) > $1,000**  
  Each customer should generate over $1,000 in lifetime value.

- **Must have made 12 sales**  
  Demonstrates real traction and product‑market validation.

- **Short sales cycle**  
  Quick decision‑to‑purchase timeframe (reduces risk and runway pressure).

- **Provided documents for marketing templates**  
  Startup has all required documentation to use the Module’s marketing resources.

- **MoneyService + revenue sharing model**  
  Utilizes the MoneyService for billing and revenue split between Member and Module.

- **Easy customer education and support**  
  Product/service must be easy for customers to use and understand (“just use the app”).

- **API integration required**  
  Offers API endpoints for automation, onboarding, or module workflows.

- **No territory restrictions**  
  Business operates without geographic exclusivity constraints.
