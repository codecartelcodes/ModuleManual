# Sourcing Sales Candidates

Relying only on job boards is usually inefficient for hiring strong salespeople because the best candidates are often passive and not actively applying. A better approach is to tap into employee networks, referrals, and trusted industry contacts.

---

## 1. Job Boards
- **WHAT:** Use sales-specific marketplaces to increase visibility.
- **HOW:** Post on sites like SalesHeads, SalesGravy, and Wellfound.
- **WHY:** These platforms can reach sales candidates and startup talent faster than general job boards.

---

## 2. Employee Networks
- **WHAT:** Ask your team for introductions and referrals.
- **HOW:** Send a quick email to employees and connections asking who they know.
- **WHY:** Employee networks can significantly expand your candidate pool and surface higher-quality referrals.

---

## 3. Founder and Advisor Networks
- **WHAT:** Use close business relationships to source candidates.
- **HOW:** Reach out to angel investors, fellow entrepreneurs, serial founders, and advisory board members.
- **WHY:** These networks often know experienced salespeople who are a strong fit for an early-stage company.

---

## 4. Startup Positioning
- **WHAT:** Present the role clearly to attract the right salesperson.
- **HOW:** Highlight compensation, commission potential, autonomy, remote work, and company mission.
- **WHY:** Top salespeople want to know what they can earn and what they are building.

---

## 5. Hiring Strategy
- **WHAT:** Focus on candidates who are entrepreneurial and commission-driven.
- **HOW:** Look for people who are comfortable with outbound sales, cold calling, and working in a startup environment.
- **WHY:** Early sales hires need confidence, independence, and a willingness to grow with the company.

---

## 6. Best Practice
- **WHAT:** Balance job board posting with direct sourcing.
- **HOW:** Use job boards for reach, but prioritize referrals and targeted outreach.
- **WHY:** This reduces time-to-hire and turnover while improving candidate quality.
