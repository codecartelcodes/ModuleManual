# Elevator Pitch & Hiring Process

---

## 1. Elevator Pitch
- **WHAT:** A short, clear value proposition for the role and company.
- **HOW:** Share what you sell, who it helps, and why it matters.
- **WHY:** Helps candidates quickly understand the opportunity and decide if it fits them.

---

## 2. Value Proposition
- **WHAT:** Why someone should work here.
- **HOW:** Highlight:
  - Competitive compensation (52% want this)
  - Work-life balance (38%)
  - Advancement opportunities (31%)
  - Training and continued education (27%)
  - Ethics and transparency (27%)
  - Work from home options (25%)
  - Mentorship (26%)
- **WHY:** These factors help attract top sales talent.

---

## 3. Sales Process Overview
- **WHAT:** Explain the sales process transparently.
- **HOW:** Share how leads are generated, how calls are tracked, and how deals move through the pipeline.
- **WHY:** Shows clarity and structure, which builds trust.

---

## 4. Expectations
- **WHAT:** Set clear rules for the role.
- **HOW:** Define:
  - Availability requirements
  - Use of SuiteCRM to log calls
  - Customer support responsibilities
  - Lead generation expectations
- **WHY:** Reduces confusion and aligns behavior with business goals.

---

## 5. Transparency
- **WHAT:** Daily reporting of activity and outcomes.
- **HOW:** Activity metrics and results are shared with management and the team.
- **WHY:** Reinforces accountability, standards, and trust.

---

## 6. Payment and Tracking
- **WHAT:** How sales reps are paid and tracked.
- **HOW:** Explain commission structure, payment schedule, and how calls and deals are tracked in CRM.
- **WHY:** Clarifies earning potential and expectations.

---

## 7. Orientation, Not an Interview
- **WHAT:** The first step is an orientation.
- **HOW:** Tell candidates you’ll cover all job aspects and expectations, and ask them to save questions for the end.
- **WHY:** Helps you qualify and set expectations at the same time.

---

## 8. Group Interview
- **WHAT:** Use group interviews to screen sales reps.
- **HOW:** Conduct a group session to see how candidates interact, speak, and handle questions.
- **WHY:** Reveals personality, energy, and attitude under pressure.

---

## 9. Entrepreneurial Mindset
- **WHAT:** Look for candidates who think like business owners.
- **HOW:** Ask “what motivates you?” and see if they can walk away from opportunities where the solution doesn’t fit.
- **WHY:** Strong salespeople know when to pursue and when to let go.

---

## 10. Values, Culture, Vision
- **WHAT:** Define what your company stands for.
- **HOW:** Share values, culture, and long-term vision.
- **WHY:** Ensures candidates align with the company’s mission and culture.
