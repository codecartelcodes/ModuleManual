# Campaign Planning Framework

## Research

- Search: “SEO deliverables”, “Link Marketing deliverables”, etc.  
- Identify common benchmarks, milestones, and deliverables  
- Determine pricing models:
  - Pay by benchmarks  
  - Pay by milestones  
  - Pay by deliverables  

---

## Payment Structure

- Align payments to **tasks or deliverables**  
- Define clear “Get Paid For” outcomes  
- Consider:
  - Flat fee  
  - Escrow-based payments  
  - Milestone releases  

---

## Campaign Definition

### WHAT

- Define campaign type (SEO, Link Marketing, Lead Generation, etc.)  
- Establish clear value proposition  

---

### WHERE

- Capture page URL  
- Platforms for posting (freelancer sites, ads, forums, etc.)  
- Location where results are:
  - Displayed  
  - Sent  
- Destination for deliverables  

---

### WHEN

- Define total project duration  
- Set expectations for completion timeline  

---

### HOW

- List required tasks  
- Define execution steps for each task  
- Establish testing methods:
  - Messaging validation  
  - Pain point alignment  
- Ensure compliance with freelancer platform TOS  
- Define pricing model (flat fee, escrow, etc.)  
- Capture insights from potential customers  
- Require lead form completion  

---

### WHO

- Define responsible entity (Module or external)  
- Identify required skills  
- Specify worker type (freelancer, specialist, etc.)  
- Define target customer:
  - Pain points  
  - Search intent  
- Ensure capture page aligns with customer needs  

---

### WHY

- Define KPIs and success criteria  
- Establish clear definition of “done”  
- Track leads:
  - Method of tracking  
  - Method of proof (dashboard, reports, etc.)  
- Consider sharing analytics (e.g., Google Analytics reports)
