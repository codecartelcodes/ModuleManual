# Hiring Your First Salesperson

---

## 1. Name
- **WHAT:** Basic candidate information.
- **HOW:** Capture name, contact info, and preferred way to communicate.
- **WHY:** Helps you track and follow up with the right person.

---

## 2. Culture Fit
- **WHAT:** Alignment with company values and team environment.
- **HOW:** Look for:
  - Entrepreneurial mindset
  - Teamwork and openness to sharing
  - Comfort with transparency and metrics
- **WHY:** Culture fit drives long-term success and retention.

---

## 3. Work Ethic
- **WHAT:** How hard and consistently the candidate works.
- **HOW:** Ask about:
  - Hours they’re willing to work
  - How they handle rejection or slow periods
  - Examples of going above and beyond
- **WHY:** Strong work ethic is critical for sales performance.

---

## 4. Experience
- **WHAT:** Past sales roles and achievements.
- **HOW:** Explore:
  - Experience selling a product or service with a similar audience
  - Ability to leverage existing networks
  - Track record of hitting or exceeding targets
- **WHY:** Experience helps shorten ramp-up time and shows pattern of success.

---

## 5. Brand Knowledge
- **WHAT:** Familiarity with your company or industry.
- **HOW:** Ask:
  - What they know about your brand
  - How they’d position your product
  - Competitors they think you compare to
- **WHY:** Brand-knowledgeable candidates onboard faster and sell more credibly.

---

## 6. Responses
- **WHAT:** How candidates respond to questions and feedback.
- **HOW:** Pay attention to:
  - Grammatical and professional tone
  - Personalization and follow-up (thank you notes, LinkedIn messages)
  - Quality of follow-up after interviews
- **WHY:** Strong responses signal professionalism and genuine interest.

---

## 7. Core Traits to Look For
- Resourcefulness: Can they figure things out on their own?
- Coachability: Do they accept feedback and improve?
- Resilience: Do they bounce back from rejection?
- Teamwork: Do they share tools, tips, and wins?
- Ambition: Are they driven to grow and succeed?
- Curiosity: Do they ask questions and seek answers?
- Feedback: Do they listen and act on advice?
- Motivation: Do they understand what drives them?
- Listening and Empathy: Do they hear and feel the customer’s needs?
- Closure: Do they know when to move on?
- Growth Mindset and Timing: Do they balance urgency with patience?

---

## 8. Traits of Great Sales DNA
- Ambition, competitiveness, urgency, confidence, perseverance, optimism, resilience, and persuasiveness.
- The right sales DNA acquires knowledge quickly, finds the right people, and builds relationships that drive revenue.

---

## 9. Entrepreneurial Fit
- Look for:
  - Self-starters who own the outcome
  - Willingness to work on commission
  - Comfort with risk and uncertainty
- Why: Entrepreneurs thrive in early-stage, high-growth environments.

---

## 10. Digital Presence
- **WHAT:** Presence on professional platforms.
- **HOW:** Verify:
  - LinkedIn profile (87% of recruiters use LinkedIn)
  - Online visibility and activity
- **WHY:** 41% of employers may skip candidates they can’t find online.
